\subsection{Controls for the prescribed-bath histories}
\label{sup:numericalcontrols}
The histories use a uniform regime-A body with a separate surface
temperature, followed by 64 uniform radial cells for both Faner and DT at
the middle time level of the convergence ladder (Sec.~\ref{sup:ladder}).
Every step of those histories is accepted under the gates of main
Sec.~\ref*{M-sec:numerics:gates}, ``Solver acceptance gates'': water,
hexane and surface rows at $10^{-10}$ with the Newton search targeting the
same value, energy rows at $\max(10^{-10},2\eta_i)$ with the row's
representability floor $\eta_i$ recorded, and the shell-birth step only
when the condition number of the column-scaled Jacobian its search solves
is below $10^{8}$. The root controls described in the paragraphs below
belong to the earlier four-cell Faner and two-cell DT histories, which the
ladder replaces; they are kept here as the record of those calculations.
Root residuals, numerical seeds and terminal-time estimates are reported
separately from the normalized component and energy acceptance limit
$10^{-10}$. Accepted-state records retain the incoming state, source
hashes, controller settings and conservation checks; refused attempts
remain available with the calculation records (Sec.~\ref{sup:deposit}).

\paragraph{Faner roots and core extinction.}
\label{sup:numericalcontrols:faner}
In the earlier four-cell Faner history, the ordinary-root controller permitted residuals up to $10^{-8}$;
the updated positive-core target solves use the regional $10^{-9}$
criterion where the stricter root stalls. The dedicated vanishing-core
mode permits $2\times10^{-8}$ only for incoming wet volume fractions
at most $10^{-7}$, with a scaled condition-number ceiling up to $10^{18}$.
Mesh-face departure seeds may use $10^{-9}$ and advance no time.
Component and energy acceptance remains $10^{-10}$, with phase and
regime-sequence checks unchanged; tighter root convergence is required
when needed to satisfy conservation.

Core extinction follows four actual shrinking-core intervals. Duration
contraction supplies an initial guess only; projection still transfers
inventories and external exchanges over its estimated duration. The
remaining-time bound assumes a stated future contraction ratio and is
below 20 ms for Faner (2 ms for DT), rather than a full-history error
bound. The updated Faner bound is 10.08 ms, with the last four durations
contracting by factors 0.68244, 0.68222 and 0.68205. Repeated targets
caused by decimal rounding are refused under the unchanged conditioning
ceiling, then replaced by the next prescribed target without changing
stored geometry.

The earlier four-cell Faner history's independent whole-history
exterior-rate accumulation used a reporting allowance $4\times10^{-10}$,
corresponding to at most $10^{-11}$ kg water/kg dry at initial water
0.025 kg/kg, distinct from the per-step $10^{-10}$ conservation gate. The
64-cell ladder histories need no such allowance: their independent
original-feed audits close at $1.2\times10^{-12}$ (Faner) and
$5.0\times10^{-12}$ (DT) against $10^{-10}$. The observed
defects are reported with the histories and are far below the loading
discrepancy. Earlier mesh and timestep comparisons remain identified as
constant-property evidence (Sec.~\ref{sup:fanerrefinement}).

\paragraph{Earlier constant-property DT recoveries.}
\label{sup:numericalcontrols:dt}
At $K_\ell=0.32$ mol/(m$^2$ s), the first shell interval reused a rejected
iterate only as a starting guess for the same problem. At the first mesh
face, a tangent residual $2.19\times10^{-10}$ exceeded the earlier
$2\times10^{-10}$ seed tolerance, so a seed-only bound
$3\times10^{-10}$ was declared. The departure solve used the neighbouring
$K_\ell=0.10$ solution for its initial guess and residual-row scaling;
the actual 0.32 state, forces and balances were solved anew. Its accepted
0.05-s step had scaled residual $9.2\times10^{-11}$. The solution-path
check retained 3\,000\,000 sampled points and all tolerances while its
wall allowance increased from 120 to 600 s per step. Mobility factors
100 and 10\,000 used these controls.

Factor 10\,000 required a separate exception at its first 0.05-s face
departure: the newborn shell water-row residual could reach $10^{-9}$
(actual $5.6\times10^{-10}$, equivalent to $4.2\times10^{-12}$ kg water/kg
dry), while every other row stayed below $10^{-10}$. Auxiliary starting
guesses were admitted up to $2\times10^{-9}$ before the full solve.
An algebraically identical liquid-water force, checked against 80-digit
arithmetic, avoided cancellation; the path check allowed 12 million points
and 1800 s. This water-row exception is the only relaxed accepted-step
row tolerance in those earlier studies; particle-wide component and energy
balances still satisfy $10^{-10}$ per step and cumulatively.

The $K_\ell=0.05$ case used a neighbouring solution as its first departure
guess and, after film depletion, adaptively reduced the regime-C step
from 5 to 1.25 s. At $K_\ell=0.20$, exact supply exhaustion gave a
transferred/requested-water ratio $1+2\times10^{-16}$ through round-off;
reordered arithmetic returned exactly one, without a clamp.

\paragraph{Earlier temperature-property histories.}
\label{sup:numericalcontrols:properties}
The former two-cell factor-100 DT histories of
Sec.~\ref{sup:temperatureproperties}, computed before the ladder, use the archived native controller
with the existing same-problem birth-iterate starting guesses and
face-departure duration retries. A failed birth advances no physical
time; accepted material from another coefficient case is not imported.
Native root criteria, three-million-point path budget, 120-s per-step
path allowance and component/energy acceptance at $10^{-10}$ are
unchanged. Each DT property alternative starts from the common original
feed and retains its own law and cumulative ledgers. The 2.5-s regime-C
check reuses only the matching factor-100 regime-B checkpoint.
The unchanged Faner property histories retain their previously
documented controls. Refused attempts and executed settings remain
with each history; results are in Sec.~\ref{sup:temperatureproperties}.
These controls do not establish whole-history continuum convergence.

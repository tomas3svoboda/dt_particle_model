\section{Governing and supporting equations with their source basis}
\label{sup:equations}
The following tables expand the main-text equation map. They are a reading
guide to the equations already defined in the two documents, with their
literature basis or derivation stated alongside. A citation to a general
framework does not identify the present coupled model or its coefficients
as a published or experimentally validated law. ``Present closure'' denotes
the formulation adopted here; a literature-informed coefficient can remain
an engineering assumption.

Throughout the tables, $\mathcal D_r(F)=r^{-2}\partial_r(r^2F)$,
$s=\front$, $z=(s/\Rp)^3$, and $[\cdot]_{\mathrm w}^{\mathrm d}$ is the
dry-side value minus the wet-side value. The region label w denotes the
liquid-hexane core; component w denotes water. $C_k$ includes all local
forms of component $k$ per particle volume, $u$ is total stored energy
per volume, and $J_E=q+\sum_k\bar h_kj_k$. Local $j_k$ is molar;
surface-film $j_k$ is mass-based and converts through $M_k$.
$y_k$ and $Y_k$ are gas mole and mass fractions, respectively.
Fluxes are outward-positive. Repeated state arguments are omitted;
the referenced equation or section supplies the full definitions and domains.

Local components\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:localcomp})
& $\partial_t C_k^\alpha+\mathcal D_r(j_k^\alpha)=0$
& Conserve water and hexane in either smooth region; phase redistribution has no separate source.
& Component conservation, with the present regional storage and fluxes.\\[5pt]
Local energy\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:localenergy})
& $\partial_t u^\alpha+\mathcal D_r(J_E^\alpha)=0$\newline
  $J_E=q+\sum_k\bar h_kj_k$
& Evolve temperature while counting each stream's enthalpy once; dry meal and oil are immobile.
& Energy conservation on one component datum; present coupling.\\[5pt]
Dry-shell storage\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:drystorage})
& $C_k^{\mathrm d}=\varepsilon_gcy_k+\Cdm W_k/M_k$
& Add pore-gas and retained inventories without including gas in $W_k$.
& Present phase-volume accounting, closed by the property laws in Table~\ref{tab:equations:closures}.\\[5pt]
Core material label\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:shellconserved})
& $\mathrm D_s(\Cdm\Xhwa)/\mathrm Dt=0$
& Preserve activation loading in surviving wet material; no relative core hexane flux.
& Present historical-core assumption; $X_{\mathrm h,w}^a=X_c(\theta_a)$ at activation.\\[5pt]
Wet-core energy\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:wetenergy})
& $\begin{aligned}
e_{\mathrm w}={}&h_{\mathrm{dm}}+X_{\mathrm w}h_{\mathrm{w,l}}\\
&+\Xhwa u_{\mathrm{h,l}}-B_{\mathrm h}(1,T,w_o)
\end{aligned}$
& Store matrix, water, historical hexane and binding energy per dry-meal mass.
& Present composite caloric closure; pure-fluid and binding sources in Table~\ref{tab:equations:closures}.\\[5pt]
Moving volumes\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:movingcomp})--(\ref*{M-eq:movingenergy})
& $\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dt}\int_V b\,\mathrm dV
  =-\int_{\partial V}(F_b-\dot r_b b)n_r\,\mathrm dA$\newline
  $b=C_k,u$; $F_b=j_k,J_E$
& Include material swept by the moving radial boundaries. Here $\dot r_b$ is their radial speed and $n_r=\pm1$.
& Moving-control-volume conservation, specialized to a sphere in the main text.\\[5pt]
Front jumps\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:rhcomp})--(\ref*{M-eq:rhenergy})
& $[j_k-\dot s C_k]_{\mathrm w}^{\mathrm d}=0$\newline
  $[J_E-\dot s u]_{\mathrm w}^{\mathrm d}=0$
& Match water, hexane and energy across the sharp front, with stored donor states in swept terms.
& Rankine--Hugoniot conservation \citep{crank1984free}; present phase closures.\\[5pt]
Front speed\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:frontvelocity})
& $\displaystyle\dot s=-\frac{[q]+\bar h_{\mathrm{w,d}}j_{\mathrm{w,d}}
                   -\bar h_{\mathrm{w,w}}j_{\mathrm{w,w}}}{\mathcal E_\Gamma}$\newline
  $\mathcal E_\Gamma=\bar h_{\mathrm{h,d}}(C_{\mathrm{h,d}}^\Gamma-C_{\mathrm{h,w}}^{\mathrm b})
                  -(u_{\mathrm d}^\Gamma-u_{\mathrm w}^{\mathrm b})$
& Heat-fed recession derived from both jumps and zero relative wet-side hexane flux; no total-vapour pressure resistance.
& Present elimination of the hexane flux. Eq.~\eqref{eq:sub:latent} explains $\mathcal E_\Gamma$.\\[5pt]
Sequential drainage\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:complementarity})
& $X_f\ge0$, $R_p-s\ge0$\newline
  $X_f(R_p-s)=0$, $\dot s\le0$
& Exhaust external free hexane before recession; admit primary drainage only.
& Present regime and admissibility conditions; violations are rejected.\\[5pt]
Shared swept volume\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:sweptvolume})
& $Q_\Gamma=V_{R_p}(z_{\mathrm{new}}-z_{\mathrm{old}})/\Delta t$
& Use one signed volume-change rate in component, energy and front equations.
& Present finite-volume geometric conservation.\\[5pt]
Retained-water capacity\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:capcomplementarity})
& $0\le W_{\mathrm{cap}}-W\ \perp\ \lambda\ge0$
& Enforce capacity as an active set; $\lambda=0$ below capacity.
& Present constraint using the source-domain retention capacity; Sec.~\ref{sup:parameters}.\\

Binary pore exchange\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:binaryflux})
& $J_{\mathrm w}=-J_{\mathrm h}=-\Lwh\nabla_{\mathrm{comp}}\Xi$\newline
  $\Xi=(\mu_{\mathrm w}-\mu_{\mathrm h})/(RT)$\newline
  $\Lwh=cy_{\mathrm w}y_{\mathrm h}D_b$
& Separate the two vapours relative to molar-average motion; the mobility vanishes at purity.
& Maxwell--Stefan framework \citep{krishna1997maxwell}; present nonideal force evaluation.\\[5pt]
Retained-water and total gas flux\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:retflux})
& $N_{\mathrm{w,ret}}=-\Lwr\nabla_{\mathrm{comp}}(\mu_{\mathrm{w,ret}}/RT)$\newline
  $N_i^{\mathrm g}=y_iN_t+J_i$
& Supply a distinct water path; recover $N_t$ algebraically from continuity. Independent Soret terms are omitted.
& Present two-path closure; retained mobility construction in Sec.~\ref{sup:finitecontact}.\\[5pt]
Nonisothermal face force\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:compositionforcepath})
& $\displaystyle G_{\mathrm{comp}}(\Xi)=\frac1d\int_0^1
 \left.\frac{\partial\Xi}{\partial y_{\mathrm h}}\right|_{T(\lambda),P}
 \frac{\mathrm dy_{\mathrm h}}{\mathrm d\lambda}\,\mathrm d\lambda$
& Integrate only the composition force along the actual interface reconstruction; ordinary faces use a common $T$.
& Present reconstruction under the negligible-Soret approximation.\\[5pt]
Pore diffusivity\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:porediffusivitytemperature})
& $\displaystyle D_b=4.0\times10^{-10}
 \left(\frac{T}{378.15\,\mathrm K}\right)^{1.75}
 \frac{101325\,\mathrm{Pa}}{P}$\newline
  $[D_b]=\mathrm{m^2/s}$
& Nominal pore-effective law; assumes constant hindrance. Prescribed magnitudes $(2$--$6)\times10^{-10}$ m$^2$/s are a sensitivity range.
& Shape: \citet{fuller1966new}. Scalar scale: \citet{cardarelli2002modeling}. Binary magnitude unvalidated; Sec.~\ref{sup:temperatureproperties}.\\[5pt]
External water and hexane\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:externalwatermass})--(\ref*{M-eq:externalwaternode})
& $\dot L_{\mathrm w}=j_{\mathrm w,p}-j_{\mathrm w,g}$\newline
  $j_{\mathrm h,p}=j_{\mathrm h,g}$\newline
  $\mathrm d(L_{\mathrm w}u_{\mathrm w,l})/\mathrm dt
     =J_{E,\mathrm p}-J_{E,\mathrm g}$
& Store condensate separately. Water appearance/depletion conserves stock and energy; hexane passes without added resistance.
& Present surface balances and declared hexane-transparent water-film limit.\\[5pt]
Gas-film components\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:gasfilmcomponents})
& $\begin{aligned}
j_{\mathrm h,g}={}&Y_{\mathrm h,s}j_t\\
 &+K_f(Y_{\mathrm h,s}-Y_{\mathrm h,b})\Phi(j_t/K_f)\\
j_{\mathrm w,g}={}&j_t-j_{\mathrm h,g}
\end{aligned}$
& Finite-flow ideal binary mass-coordinate film, using equation-of-state phase traces.
& Analytic film closure adopted here; heat/mass exposure convention in Sec.~\ref{sup:detail:geometry}.\\[5pt]
Ackermann correction\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:ackermannfactor})
& $\Phi(x)=x/(e^x-1)$, $\Phi(0)=1$\newline
  $\displaystyle\beta=\frac{j_{\mathrm w,g}c_{p,\mathrm w}^{f}
                   +j_{\mathrm h,g}c_{p,\mathrm h}^{f}}{h_Q}$
& Both signed component capacity fluxes enter, including opposing flows; declared band $|\beta|\le3$.
& Adopted analytic finite-flow correction; the wider film-law band remains an assumption.\\[5pt]
Gas-film energy\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:gasfilmenergy})
& $q_{\mathrm{in}}=h_Q\Phi(\beta)(T_{\mathrm{bulk}}-T_s)$\newline
  $\displaystyle J_{E,\mathrm g}=-q_{\mathrm{in}}
      +\sum_k(j_{k,\mathrm g}/M_k)\bar h_k(T_s)$
& Combine sensible heat and stream enthalpy on the same datum; no added latent source.
& Present energy coupling; bed coefficient from \citet{faner2008desolventizado}.\\[5pt]
Finite liquid entry\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:finitecontact})
& $G_\ell=(d/\mathcal L_\ell+1/K_\ell)^{-1}$\newline
  $N_\ell=-G_\ell(\psi_s-\psi_p)$, $\psi=\ln a_{\mathrm{w,ret}}$
& Contact and interior resistances in series; active only in a liquid-connected dry shell. Donor energy follows the water.
& Present effective closure. Mobility and contact constructions in Sec.~\ref{sup:contactscale} and Table~\ref{tab:equations:support}.\\[5pt]
Finite film supply\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:liquidstocksupply})
& $\begin{aligned}
j_{\ell,\mathrm{in}}\le\max\bigl(0,{}&L_{\mathrm w,old}/\Delta t\\
 &+j_{\mathrm w,p}^{\mathrm{base}}-j_{\mathrm w,g}\bigr)
\end{aligned}$
& Bound inward addition by stored and newly supplied water; debit film and credit particle equally.
& Present discrete stock-availability condition.\\[5pt]
Native-meal hexane retention\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:gab})
& $\displaystyle W_{\mathrm{native}}=\frac{X_m Cx}{(1-x)(1-x+Cx)}$\newline
  $x=K(T)a_{\mathrm h}$
& GAB total-meal isotherm; the reference sample already includes residual oil.
& Soybean-meal fit of \citet{cardarelli1996hexane}; temperature-dependent $C$ and $K$.\\[5pt]
Oil anchoring\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:oilanchorcorrection})
& $W_{\mathrm h}=\alpha_oW_{\mathrm{h,native}}+\beta_oq_o$\newline
  $\alpha_o=(1-w_o)/(1-w_{o,\mathrm{ref}})$\newline
  $\beta_o=(w_o-w_{o,\mathrm{ref}})/(1-w_{o,\mathrm{ref}})$\newline
  $q_o=0.9635a_{\mathrm h}^{2.7036}$
& Adjust off-reference oil content without duplicating reference oil. Use the same weights for sensible heat.
& Present reference-anchored composite construction; source and comparison convention in Sec.~\ref{sup:oilmap}.\\[5pt]
Retained water\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:luikov})
& $W_{\mathrm w}=A_1/[1+A_2\ln(1/a_{\mathrm w})]$\newline
  $\ln a_{\mathrm w}=(1-A_1/W_{\mathrm w})/A_2$
& Temperature-independent nominal isotherm. Positive low-water extension is declared; no lower water floor.
& Modified Luikov fit \citep{luz2006isotermas,pixton1975moisture,luikov1975systems}; source limits in main Sec.~\ref*{M-sec:thermo:properties}.\\[5pt]
Oil heat capacity\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:oilheatcapacity})
& $c_{p,\mathrm o}(T)=1024+3T$ J/(kg K)
& Soybean-oil sensible capacity; $T$ in kelvin, reported range 299.15--433.15 K.
& Rounded linear fit, Table 7 of \citet{garciarojas2013thermophysical}.\\[5pt]
Dry-matrix sensible energy\newline
(\ref*{M-eq:drymatrixsensibleenergy})
& $\begin{aligned}
\Delta u_{\mathrm{dm}}={}&\alpha_oc_{p,\mathrm{native}}(T-T_r)\\
 &+\beta_o[1024(T-T_r)\\
 &\qquad+1.5(T^2-T_r^2)]
\end{aligned}$
& Analytic integral of the same capacity used for the temperature derivative. $T_r$ denotes the solid reference $T_s$ of the full equation.
& Integration of the oil fit with present reference anchoring; oil correction cancels at $w_o=0.0195$.\\[5pt]
Fluid properties and equilibrium\newline
Sec.~\ref*{M-sec:thermo:properties}
& $f_{i,\mathrm g}=f_{i,\mathrm l}$ at an admitted interface\newline
  $\bar h_{i,\mathrm g}=h_{i,\mathrm g}^{\mathrm{ideal}}+\bar h_{i,\mathrm g}^{\mathrm{res}}$
& Close liquid density, fugacities and caloric values. Binary gas uses a second-virial mixture with a cross term.
& Hexane: \citet{span2003eos1,span2003eos2}; water: \citet{wagner2002iapws,iapws2018r695}; mixture: \citet{plyasunov2003crossvirial}.\\[5pt]
Hexane binding energy\newline
Sec.~\ref{sup:binding}
& $\displaystyle\psi_{\mathrm h}=\frac{RT}{M_{\mathrm h}}\int_0^W
                          \ln a_{\mathrm h}(\xi,T)\,\mathrm d\xi$\newline
  $B_{\mathrm h}=T\partial_T\psi_{\mathrm h}-\psi_{\mathrm h}$
& Derive retained-hexane calorics from the same retention law; no independently fitted sorption-heat source.
& Present thermodynamic construction from the GAB activity law \citep{cardarelli1996hexane}.\\

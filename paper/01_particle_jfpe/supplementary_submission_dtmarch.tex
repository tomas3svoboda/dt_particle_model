\section{The prescribed DT bath and particle history}
\label{sup:dtmarch}
This section records the prescribed DT bath and resulting particle
history. Shared constitutive laws and surface assumptions are those of
main Sec.~\ref*{M-sec:model}; tray vapour and energy balances are not
solved in this experiment.

\paragraph{Inputs.}
The prescribed residence is 1766.535501 s and the effective spherical
radius is 1.5 mm. This is a selected coarse-particle case; the main text
states its origin and distinguishes it from a measured DT mean radius.
Feed temperature is 322.15 K, hexane loading 0.387786 kg/kg dry meal,
retained water 0.123664122 kg/kg dry meal and oil fraction 0.013740458.
The pre-desolventizing (PD) bath is 345.15 K, with hexane mole fraction
0.6644429974, exposure fraction 0.004081 and indirect duty 85 W/kg dry
until 490.202242 s. The steam-rich bath then ends at 378.15 K;
its gas states and timings are given in the prescribed-bath input file
of the release (Sec.~\ref{sup:deposit}). These inputs define the prescribed-bath scenario. Comparison with
plant dynamics requires measured tray histories or a coupled column calculation.

The common material inputs are in main Table~\ref*{M-tab:parameters};
DT imbibition coefficients are constructed in Sec.~\ref{sup:finitecontact}.
The spatial representation and numerical controls are specified
in Sec.~\ref{sup:numericalcontrols}.

\paragraph{Events and exit.}
The values below are those of the 64-cell history at the middle time
level of the convergence ladder (Sec.~\ref{sup:ladder}), which the main
text reports. External free hexane is depleted at 492.257251 s, and the
first dry-shell interval ends at 492.312251 s. Regime B resolves the
front's grid-face arrivals and departures before core extinction at
502.587745 s. The external water film stays positive throughout regime B.
Regime C advances the stationary radial particle and water film together,
resolves film depletion at 552.629015 s, about one minute after dry-shell
formation, and continues to the prescribed exit at 1766.535501 s. Final
core volume and external water film are zero. Particle water is
0.234328355 kg/kg dry, hexane is 0.000157928910 kg/kg dry and volume-mean
temperature is 377.861233 K.

Wet-basis moisture is $100X_{\mathrm w}/(1+X_{\mathrm w}+X_{\mathrm h})$,
giving 18.9819\%. Residual hexane is 157.929 mg/kg dry, or 127.931 ppm
on a wet basis with the same denominator. The earlier two-cell history,
computed before the retained-water continuation, gave 17.4618\%, 138.389 ppm and
film depletion at 703.26 s; with the present code the two-cell rung gives
18.32\%, 112.5 ppm and film depletion at 573.3 s, and its distance from
64 cells is in Table~\ref{tab:sub:ladderdt}. This conversion excludes the water
stored in the external film. Figure~\ref*{M-fig:dtmarch} of the main text
places the published outlet ranges at 30 min for visual reference; the
computed clock and its earlier end point are not shifted. The published ranges place the particle result in industrial context;
direct validation requires observations matched to the particle and bath.

\paragraph{Conservation and numerical scope.}
An independent reconstruction covers every accepted step of the 64-cell
history from the original feed to exit, evaluating water, hexane and
energy in the particle and external water film. The maximum normalized
defects are $5.02\times10^{-12}$, $3.59\times10^{-13}$ and
$2.04\times10^{-12}$, respectively, against the declared $10^{-10}$.
Within it, the feed history up to dry-shell activation, regenerated with
the integrated oil law, gives maximum normalized water, hexane and energy
defects $1.58\times10^{-12}$, $3.45\times10^{-13}$ and
$9.4\times10^{-14}$. The feed does not depend on the pore diffusivity
because regime A contains no binary pore transport. Its inputs and
boundary are held fixed.
Mesh and time-step refinement of this history is in Sec.~\ref{sup:ladder};
the earlier regime-C step check of the two-cell history is in
Sec.~\ref{sup:temperatureproperties}, and its regime-B recoveries in
Sec.~\ref{sup:numericalcontrols:properties}.

The convergence ladder establishes mesh and time-step convergence of the
outlet hexane and temperature at 64 cells; the outlet moisture converges
at an order of about one half under a thin surface diffusion layer, and a
surface-graded mesh is the next refinement (Sec.~\ref{sup:ladder}).
The regime-B trajectory retains a positive water film throughout, so assessing
the depleted-film branch requires a separate case.

Each segment records the hashes of its source files and of the saved
state from which it starts. The updated histories use one isolated native-source
snapshot, with an explicit property-law adapter and recorded numerical controllers. The release
(Sec.~\ref{sup:deposit}) provides sources, inputs and histories for tracing
the reported calculation. The balance and numerical checks characterize this
discrete particle experiment. Identification of its material coefficients
and assessment at equipment scale require independent particle and process
measurements.
